Welcome to Cupful

Cupful is a social app designed to help people meet, connect and arrange coffee meetups. We believe that good conversations can start with a simple cup of coffee.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026

Cupful respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we handle, why we use it and how you can exercise your privacy rights.

1. Who is responsible for your information?

Cupful is operated by Michelle Federipe.

Contact: support.cupful@gmail.com

2. Information we collect

Depending on how you use Cupful, information may include:

  • Your account details and email address.
  • Your profile information and spoken languages.
  • Profile photographs you upload.
  • Location information you provide or save for nearby matching and café discovery.
  • Matches, connections and meetup information.
  • Messages and conversations with other members.
  • Reports, blocks and feedback you submit.
  • Device push notification tokens and notification preferences.

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Create and manage your account.
  • Display your member profile.
  • Help you discover and connect with other members.
  • Arrange coffee invitations and meetups.
  • Find cafés near your saved location.
  • Send relevant notifications.
  • Respond to support requests and safety reports.
  • Maintain and protect the app.

4. Messages and information shared with members

Information in your profile may be visible to other members as part of the matching experience. Messages and meetup details are shared with the members involved in those conversations or meetups.

5. Location information

Cupful uses location information you provide or save to support nearby member matching and café discovery. You can manage location permissions through your device settings. Café searches may use OpenStreetMap services to find nearby places.

6. Service providers

Cupful uses third-party services to operate the app. These include:

  • Supabase for account authentication, database and file storage.
  • Expo for app push notification services.
  • OpenStreetMap services for café discovery.
  • Apple services for app distribution and related platform functions.

These providers may process information as necessary to provide their services, subject to their own privacy terms.

7. Data retention and account deletion

We keep personal information while it is needed to provide the service and manage your account. When you delete your account, associated profile information, photos, location records, conversations, messages, meetup records and other associated account data are deleted from our active systems.

Deleting your account also removes shared conversation and meetup history involving you, which means other participants may lose access to that history too. Copies in service-provider backups may remain for a limited period under the provider's retention processes.

8. Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, including the GDPR, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, object to certain processing and request data portability.

To exercise your rights, contact support.cupful@gmail.com.

If you are in the Netherlands, you may also contact the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

9. Security

We use technical and organisational measures intended to protect account information. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when Cupful changes or when necessary to reflect legal requirements. The latest version will be published on this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, contact support.cupful@gmail.com.

Account Deletion

You can permanently delete your Cupful account through the app using the Delete Account option in your profile.

You will be asked to confirm your password before deletion.

Deleting your account removes your associated profile, photographs, conversations, messages and meetup history. Please note that shared conversations and meetup history involving you will also be deleted for the other participants.

If you cannot access the app or need assistance with account deletion, contact: support.cupful@gmail.com .
